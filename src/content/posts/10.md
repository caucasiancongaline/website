"$0" is a song that I listened to multiple times and I always thought it interesting, but it was often just in one ear and out the other. That changed last Sunday as I walked through Central Park on a sweltering Sunday which had cooled just enough by sunset. That song really came alive and suddenly I felt everything this song was supposed to make a person feel. I don't know what particular set of circumstances occured that finally allowed me to appreciate the song for what it's fully worth. That makes me think of how many other songs are out there in purgatory, waiting for me to give it another thought. Anyways, as Cameron starts belting "God is real", I was just passing under the Gothic Bridge north of the reservoir. A rat scurried out from under the bushes, stopped for a second right when I stopped, and the scurried away to the other side. It was rather strange. Rats don't usually take a second to pause in my experience. But this one did. It was a rather strange moment but was quite apt for the situation. Cameron Winter's music is very, very beautiful, but also objectively a little bit grating and strange. If nothing else, this was a perfect New York moment, at least for my version of New York.

A few strides later I was greeted by this beatiful view looking southwards on the reservoir; the pink hazy skies as the backdrop to the Midtown towers, and right below everything else in the field of view, right in front of me, was a thick bush of these beautiful yellow flowers. Google says that they're coneflowers, I don't know if I've ever noticed them before anywhere. I thought about taking a picture, but every photo I take of nature around the park comes out disapointing. That's alright, I'll remember it.
